01 / CONTROL CENTRE

National noise intelligence

Preliminary road traffic noise-risk screening for The Gambia. The application combines mapped road hierarchy, road proximity and census EA population attributes to identify areas requiring more detailed environmental noise assessment.

This is a spatial screening model, not a measured decibel map.

14 / METHODOLOGY

1. Retrieve OSM road geometries within the requested extent.

2. Load census EA polygons and population attributes.

3. Calculate EA centroids and nearest mapped road distances.

4. Assign a screening score using road hierarchy and distance.

5. Summarise the population of EAs in each screening category.

6. Export EA-level screening results for further GIS analysis.

15 / LIMITATIONS

This is NOT an acoustic propagation model. The model does not calculate Lden, Lnight, Leq, sound pressure, vehicle counts, traffic speed, road surface, topography, building façade exposure, meteorological effects or sound attenuation. Population figures represent whole EA populations in screened enumeration areas, not the number of people proven to be exposed to road noise. A measured or calibrated acoustic model is required before reporting population above 45, 50, 53 or 55 dB.
